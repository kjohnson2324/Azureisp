# Story Bible (original IP)

**Premise.** The Atlas Agency is a secret network of junior agents. Its rival is the
**Magpie Ring**, a crew who "borrow" the world's landmarks because they can't resist
shiny, famous things. Agents follow a trail of geography clues, pick the right next
country, and name the culprit. A solved case returns the landmark home and earns a passport stamp.

Tone: silly and warm, never scary. Nobody is ever hurt. Thieves are "caught", not punished,
and every case ends with them helping put the landmark back.

## The Agency

| Character | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Chief Wren** | Agency director | A small, brisk owl in reading glasses. Gives briefings and celebrates wins. |
| **Gizmo** | Field gadget | A talking pocket compass. Delivers hints, and costs points to ask. |
| **You** | Junior Agent | App-generated agent name, e.g. *Agent BraveOtter42*. Customisable trench coat, hat, badge. |

## The Magpie Ring

| Villain | Signature | Tell-tale clues |
|---|---|---|
| **Velvet Vex** | Ringleader, purple velvet cape | Leaves magpie-feather calling cards; hums opera |
| **Professor Pocket** | Coat with 100 pockets | Brass pocket watch; always running late |
| **Lady Lumen** | Collects anything that glows | Carries a lantern; sunglasses at night |
| **Sticky Fitz** | Climber, gecko gloves | Leaves smudgy handprints; loves rooftop views |
| **Dot Dash** | Speedy messenger on roller skates | Taps messages in code; drops skate-wheel tracks |

## Art direction

Flat, bold shapes with thick ink outlines (helps low-vision players and colourblind palettes).
Characters are animals or stylised people with varied skin tones. Landmarks are illustrated
in-house, not photos, when a licensed photo isn't available.
